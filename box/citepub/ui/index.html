Brief

Every case citation is checked twice: against a New York and Second Circuit law library, then against a copy of CourtListener's nationwide index. Quotes are string matches against the opinion's own text. New York statute sections are checked too. No AI model decides anything here: the optional passage suggestions come from a local model, and a suggestion is shown only if its exact words are in the opinion. You review every flag.

Privacy: your brief is checked in memory and not stored. The check runs on one private machine and calls no outside service. Not legal advice: a check, not a guarantee — confirm every flag yourself.

NOT FOUND means neither library has the citation — a coverage statement, not a fabrication verdict. Many unpublished, Westlaw-only and state decisions are missing. Confirm in Westlaw or Lexis.
